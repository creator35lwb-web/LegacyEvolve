# LegacyEvolve Sustainability Roadmap

## Vision

**LegacyEvolve Protocol (LEP) and Multi-Agent Communication Protocol (MACP) will become sustainable Digital Public Goods** that serve the global community for decades, not just years.

2. **Community Sustainability:** Active, engaged contributors
3. **Technical Sustainability:** Maintainable, scalable codebase

## Ethical Sustainability

### L (GODEL) Ethical Operating Framework

**Principles:**

1. **Transparency:** All decisions documented publicly
2. **Accountability:** Clear ownership and responsibility
3. **Fairness:** No discrimination, bias, or exclusion
4. **Privacy:** Protect user data and privacy
5. **Safety:** Prevent harm and misuse
6. **Public Good:** Prioritize societal benefit over profit

### Ethical Review Process

**For Major Changes:**

1. **Ethical Impact Assessment:** Evaluate potential harms
2. **Stakeholder Consultation:** Gather diverse perspectives
3. **FLYWHEEL Validation:** Multi-agent ethical review
4. **Public Comment:** 30-day comment period
5. **Steering Committee Approval:** Final decision

### Alignment with Digital Public Good Standards

**DPGA Criteria:**

- ✅ Open license (MIT)
- ✅ Clear ownership (Alton Lee, L GODEL)
- ✅ Platform independence
- ✅ Documentation
- ✅ Extractable data
- ✅ Privacy and security
- ✅ Inappropriate content prevention
- ✅ Adherence to standards
- ✅ Do no harm by design

## Risk Management

### Identified Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| **Maintainer Burnout** | High | Critical | Workload management, succession planning |
| **Funding Gap** | Medium | High | Diversified funding, grants |
| **Security Breach** | Low | Critical | Security scanning, audits |
| **Low Adoption** | Medium | High | Early adopter program, marketing |
| **Technical Debt** | Medium | Medium | 20% time allocation, quarterly review |

### Contingency Plans

#### 1. **Funding Gap**

- Reduce scope (focus on core features)
- Seek emergency grants
- Launch crowdfunding campaign

#### 2. **Maintainer Burnout**

- Recruit new maintainers
- Reduce release frequency
- Pause non-critical features

#### 3. **Security Breach**

- Activate incident response plan
- Publish security advisory
- Release emergency patch
